# E — Hybrid plasma models

`E1_hybrid_PIC_weak_comet.ipynb`

A 2-D hybrid code (particle ions, massless isothermal electron fluid, generalised Ohm's law with Hall and
ambipolar terms, sub-cycled Faraday solver, Boris pusher, CIC weighting). It simulates water-ion pickup and
solar-wind deflection at a weakly outgassing comet. Functions map one-to-one onto Menura's kernels.
Codes in the literature: A.I.K.E.F. (Müller et al. 2011), Menura (Behar et al. 2022), Bagdonat & Motschmann (2002).
