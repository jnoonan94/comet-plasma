# D — Fluid plasma models

`D1_fluid_mass_loaded_MHD.ipynb`

* **Part 1:** steady ion continuity in a Haser coma (ionisation, transport, dissociative recombination), checked against Goetz et al. (2022) Eqs. 9–12.
* **Part 2:** 1-D MUSCL–Rusanov finite-volume MHD with mass-loading source terms (the BATS-R-US approach of Gombosi et al. 1996). Checked against the exact steady solution; shows shock formation above the critical mass loading.
* **Part 3:** what multi-fluid and 3-D production codes add (Rubin et al. 2014; Huang et al. 2016).
