# F — Fully kinetic (PIC) plasma models

`F1_fully_kinetic_PIC_ambipolar.ipynb`

A 1-D electrostatic particle-in-cell code (particle electrons and ions, Poisson solver, leapfrog),
validated on the plasma oscillation. It is then used to grow the ambipolar potential around an ionising
cometary source at mass ratios 16, 64 and 256, extrapolated to H₂O⁺. Codes in the literature: iPIC3D
(Markidis et al. 2010) as used by Deca et al. (2017, 2019), Sishtla et al. (2019), Divin et al. (2020).
